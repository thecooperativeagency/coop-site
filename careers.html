The work

You start with inventory photography and social media content for our dealerships. Cars on the lot, shot well, posted fast. It's real volume with a real bar, and it teaches you how the whole machine runs.

We're a small team, and small teams don't do lanes. If you can write, design, cut video, build email, or think in systems, there's more waiting: magazines, websites, paid campaigns, other brands entirely. More and more of our work runs through AI and the apps we build ourselves. You should want in on that. You won't get all of it day one. You'll get as much as you can carry.

Who we want

Someone inspired, hardworking, and a little entrepreneurial.

  • You're willing to shoot inventory photography.
  • You're very good at social media content.
  • You have a body of work to show. Self-taught is fine. Your feed is your résumé.
  • The AI and app side of what we do excites you.

We'll hand you real responsibility, not meeting notes and coffee runs. You must be 21 or older. Living in or near Baton Rouge, Louisiana is a plus, but your work matters more than your résumé.
