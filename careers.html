Join a team that actually moves the needle.

The Cooperative Agency is a Baton Rouge team serving Louisiana and the surrounding area. We run the whole marketing and sales engine for car dealerships — and a growing set of other brands — and we're deep in building AI systems for ourselves and our clients, custom-building apps and software as we and they need it. We're hiring someone to own a piece of it and ship every day.

  • Social that ships
  • Video + drone
  • Email + web
  • Paid + systems
  • AI + apps

The work

You start with the cash engine: inventory photography and social content for our dealerships. Cars on the lot, shot well, posted fast. It's real volume with a real bar, and it teaches you how the whole machine runs. But we're a small team, and small teams don't do lanes. If you can write, design, cut video, build email, or think in systems, there's work waiting — magazines, websites, paid campaigns, other brands entirely. And more and more of our work runs through AI and the apps we build ourselves, so you should be excited to get your hands on that side too. You won't get all of it day one. You'll get as much as you can carry.

Who we want

Someone inspired, hardworking, and a little entrepreneurial. You already shoot, edit, and post — self-taught is fine, your feed is your résumé. Specifically: you're willing to shoot inventory photography, you're very good at social content, you have a body of work to show, and the AI and app side of what we do excites you rather than scares you. We'll hand you real responsibility, not meeting notes and coffee runs. If you're collecting internships for a line on a résumé, this isn't it. If you want your work in the wild, it is. LSU or the Baton Rouge area is a plus, but the work matters more than the major. College intern setups work here too — credit, flexible scheduling, the whole path — if that's your situation.
