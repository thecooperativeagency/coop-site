The Cooperative Agency SMS Program

The Cooperative Agency SMS Program. By opting in on our website, you agree to receive text messages from The Cooperative Agency, LLC about your inquiry, follow-ups, scheduling, and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact lance@thecoopbrla.com or (225) 240-4570. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy at thecoopbrla.com/privacy.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. The Privacy Policy states that all other data-sharing categories exclude it.

How you opt in

On the contact form at thecoopbrla.com/contact, under Mobile Phone, an optional checkbox is unchecked unless you check it. The form can be submitted without checking it. Only someone who checks that box agrees to these texts. Checking it is not required to submit the form or to buy anything.

The checkbox reads: Yes, I agree to receive text messages from The Cooperative Agency about my inquiry, including replies, follow-ups, and scheduling, at the mobile number provided. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Opt-in confirmation

After you opt in, the first message is: The Cooperative Agency: Thanks for reaching out! You're subscribed to texts about your inquiry. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Opt back in by replying START, YES, or UNSTOP.

Help

Reply HELP or INFO. The reply is: The Cooperative Agency: For help, email lance@thecoopbrla.com or call (225) 240-4570. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

You can also email lance@thecoopbrla.com or call (225) 240-4570.

Opt out

Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE, or OPTOUT. The reply is: The Cooperative Agency: You've been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe.
