This policy describes how The Cooperative Agency LLC (“we”) handles personal information collected through thecoopbrla.com, including the contact form, chat, careers application, and the phone number published on this site.

Text Messaging (SMS)

Text Messaging (SMS). If you opt in to receive text messages from The Cooperative Agency, LLC, we collect your mobile number and your consent record (date, time, and source of opt-in) to send messages about your inquiry and our services. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, and all other data-sharing categories in this policy exclude it. We may share information only with service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our SMS platform provider), and only for that purpose. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Questions: lance@thecoopbrla.com, (225) 240-4570, The Cooperative Agency, LLC, Baton Rouge, LA.

Program terms are on our SMS Terms page (thecoopbrla.com/sms-terms). The consent checkbox on the contact form is optional and is not pre-checked. You can submit that form without agreeing to texts, and consent is not a condition of purchase.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: first name, last name, dealership name, website, email, mobile phone, subject, and message.
  • SMS consent, when the contact form shows the checkbox: whether you checked it, the exact consent wording and version shown, the date and time of the submission, and the page address. When our server receives them, we also keep the IP address and the browser user agent for that submission. We already store a hash of the IP address with each lead.
  • Careers application: name, email, mobile phone, portfolio or website, social links, work sample, interests, confirmation that you are 21 or older, and your note.
  • Chat: the conversation and any name, dealership, email, phone, or note you choose to leave.
  • Phone calls to the number on this site: the details our phone service records so we can follow up.
  • Technical data our host records when you load a page, such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, and we do not sell information to advertisers.

How we use information

We use it to answer an inquiry, schedule a conversation, provide the marketing and sales-system services you ask about, review a job application, keep a record of SMS consent, and protect the site from spam and abuse.

How we share information

We use service providers to host this website, send email, and handle phone calls. They process the information they need for that work and may not use it for their own marketing. We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Mobile numbers collected for texting, and SMS opt-in data and consent, are not included in the sharing described in this section. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, and all other data-sharing categories in this policy exclude it. The only sharing of that information is with the service provider that delivers the messages, and only so those messages can be sent, as described under Text Messaging (SMS).

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry, application, and consent records for as long as we need them to respond, do the work, and show what was agreed, and then we delete or de-identify them. Email lance@thecoopbrla.com to ask about a record we have about you.

Your choices

You can submit the contact form without checking the SMS box. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Email lance@thecoopbrla.com or call (225) 240-4570 with a privacy question.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
