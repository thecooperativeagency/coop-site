02

Newsletters and offers that live on your site

Our lifestyle newsletters go out by email and feature your specials, but they give people a reason to read that isn't just buying a car: where to eat and drink around town, the coffee worth the drive, the music coming up this month. Offers-only emails really matter only to people in the upgrade cycle who are actively shopping. The lifestyle newsletter keeps everyone else reading, so your store stays top of mind until they're ready. And every send gets its own page on your website, so customers can click through, Google can find it and your sales team has one link to text.

  • Lifestyle newsletters by email: local food and drink, coffee, music and events, with your specials inside
  • Monthly offer emails for shoppers in the upgrade cycle
  • A matching page on your site for every send
