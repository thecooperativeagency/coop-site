Dealer marketing products and services, from Baton Rouge, Louisiana

The Cooperative Agency, based in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, helps car dealerships and auto groups across the South at every step from first search to sold. To get stores found, we handle search engine optimization (SEO) for inventory, service, specials, model-launch and buyer's guide pages, along with AI search optimization and AI readiness, so search engines, AI assistants and Google's AI answers can not only find a dealer's site but actually read it, use it and recommend the store. Those pages are built as a resource for people and AI alike. We also manage the dealer's Google Business Profile and keep it current with fresh update posts. For social, we come on site and into the area and shoot the lot, staff, inventory and the places around the store, so the content looks familiar to local customers, not the same factory photos every store uses. That includes on-lot photo and video, reels, walkarounds, short videos and drone, plus graphics that meet the manufacturer's brand rules. Social is planned by department, timed to the store's promotions, and every post is reviewed before it goes live. We can buy ads on Google and Meta, or manage the buy through someone else, depending on what the store has going on, including ad creative when that's what's needed. We're also piloting an AI shopping assistant feed that works alongside the dealer's site: it makes inventory, prices and specials readable to AI shopping assistants, so they can answer shoppers with the store's real cars and send the lead to the dealer.

When shoppers land, we build the pages they actually land on: feature, model and buyer's guide pages that explain lease, finance or buy in plain language; specials, service and loyalty pages, kept current with every offer; and website creative on whatever platform the site runs on. We get the Google Business Profile ready for shoppers who call or get directions straight from search, and we fix lead capture by finding the spots where forms, calls and chats slip through and closing them. For a smaller independent store, we can build the whole site, too.

To keep them close, we set up and fix customer relationship management (CRM) and follow-up, so leads land with the right person and nobody goes cold because one vendor's system didn't talk to another's. We write lifestyle newsletters people have a reason to open: where to eat and drink around town, the coffee worth the drive, the music coming up. The store's specials ride along inside, so the store stays top of mind until they're ready. We also send monthly specials, sales-event, and service and parts offer emails, each with a matching page on the dealer's site so customers can click through, Google can find it and the sales team has one link to text. When we're buying the store's ads, we run retargeting ads on Google and Meta that keep the store in front of shoppers who've already visited.

To close the loop, we deliver a one-page weekly general manager report covering traffic, inventory, leads, response times and close rates, then sit down with the store and turn it into what to do next. We coordinate vendors, cut the overlap and hold them accountable, so the store stops paying for what doesn't work. We review marketing spend so dollars move to what's actually selling cars, and we build custom AI tools shaped around how the store sells and services cars. We consult on manufacturer co-op. Think of us as an outsourced marketing director and tech lead, with one team and one point of contact for the whole path.

We look forward to working with you!
