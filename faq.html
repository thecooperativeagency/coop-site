Frequently asked questions.

What does The Cooperative Agency do?

We’re a full-service marketing and sales systems consultancy specializing in automotive dealerships. From outsourced CTO and Marketing Director services to custom content creation, vendor coordination, and co-op management — we help you streamline operations and drive more sales, all for less than the cost of a single full-time employee.

How is The Cooperative different from traditional automotive marketing agencies?

Most agencies are bloated, slow, and rely on templated, cookie-cutter content. We’re fast, local, and laser-focused on custom content that actually connects with your customers. You’ll never get generic stock photos or recycled ad copy from us.

Can you help with manufacturer co-op claims?

Yes — every service we provide is structured to maximize co-op eligibility. We tailor your strategy, creative, and deployment with OEM compliance in mind and even help submit your claims.

What’s your turnaround time on campaigns and creative?

Fast. While traditional agencies can take weeks, we deploy within hours when needed — especially for sales events, incentives, and urgent updates.

Do you manage social media for dealerships?

Absolutely. We handle local photo and video shoots, reels, strategic scheduling around promotions, OEM-aligned messaging, and consistent multi-platform branding.

What kind of content do you produce?

Localized photography and video, reels and walkarounds, OEM-compliant graphics, Google Business updates, website creative, and ad platform assets — all customized to your dealership, community, and goals.

Can you help fix lead and CRM issues?

Yes. We’re not just marketers — we’re systems people. We diagnose and resolve issues with lead flow, CRM setup, follow-up processes, and more.

Can you manage all our vendors?

That’s one of our specialties. We simplify the chaos by becoming your single point of contact, eliminate redundancies, and streamline communications.

Are your services eligible for co-op reimbursement?

Yes, most of them are. Many clients claim back 50% to 100% of our service costs.

Where do you provide services?

We work with dealerships in the southern region and pride ourselves on keeping it local — boots-on-the-ground content and regionalized strategies.

What types of dealers do you work with?

We’ve worked with premium and luxury brands including Audi, BMW, and multi-location auto groups — and the model scales across rooftops.

Do you only work with automotive dealerships?

Automotive is our specialty, but we also work with other industries. We’re expanding into small-business startups and restaurants. More info coming soon — reach out for early access.
