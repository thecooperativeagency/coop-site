FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does The Cooperative Agency do?

We’re a full-service marketing and sales systems consultancy specializing in automotive dealerships. From outsourced CTO and Marketing Director services to custom content, vendor coordination, and co-op management — we help streamline operations and drive more sales, often for less than the cost of a single full-time employee.

How is The Cooperative different from traditional automotive marketing agencies?

Most agencies are bloated, slow, and rely on templated content. We’re fast, local, and focused on custom work that actually connects. No generic stock photos or recycled ad copy — everything is built for your brand, team, and market.

Can you help with manufacturer co-op claims?

Yes. Services are structured to maximize co-op eligibility. We tailor strategy, creative, and deployment with OEM compliance in mind and help submit claims so you keep brand voice and get reimbursed.

What’s your turnaround on campaigns and creative?

Fast. Traditional agencies can take weeks; we deploy within hours when needed — especially for sales events, incentives, and urgent updates. Creative is built in-house so there’s no middleman delay.

Do you manage social media for dealerships?

Absolutely — local photo and video, reels, strategic scheduling around promotions, OEM-aligned messaging, and consistent multi-platform branding.

What kind of content do you produce?

Localized photography and video, reels and walkarounds, OEM-compliant graphics, Google Business updates, website creative, and ad platform assets — customized to your dealership and market.

Can you help fix lead and CRM issues?

Yes. We’re systems people as well as marketers. We diagnose and resolve lead flow, CRM setup, and follow-up process issues so opportunities don’t slip.

Can you manage all our vendors?

That’s a specialty. We become your single point of contact, coordinate vendors, eliminate redundancies, and streamline communications so systems actually work together.

Are your services eligible for co-op reimbursement?

Most are. We work with you and your OEM to maximize eligibility and tailor creative to brand guidelines. Many clients claim back 50% to 100% of service costs.

Where do you provide services?

We work with dealerships in the southern region and pride ourselves on keeping it local — boots-on-the-ground content, regionalized strategies, fast and flexible service.

What types of dealers do you work with?

We’ve worked with premium and luxury brands including Audi, BMW, and multi-location groups — and the model scales across rooftops, single-point or group.

Do you only work with automotive dealerships?

Automotive is our specialty, but we also support other industries. We’re expanding into small-business startups (sites, brand, go-to-market) and restaurants (websites, menu content, photography, social). Reach out for early access.
