# Findability

**Product name:** Findability  
**One-line pitch:** Premium local search engine optimization (SEO) and artificial intelligence (AI) search optimization so the store shows up where shoppers actually look — Google and AI answers — using the questions customers already ask.

## Who it's for
Automotive rooftops (and groups) that want more than a generic SEO retainer: real local pages, real store facts, and files assistants can read, without stuffing prices into those files.

## What's included
- Store facts that stay true: name, address with city and state, department phones and hours, service area
- Model and journey pages written for how people search in that market
- An `llms.txt` (and related crawler files) that describes the store in plain language and links to live pages — evergreen, no rates or expiration dates
- Structured questions and answers that match what’s on the page
- Inventory pointed at search pages, not a list of individual cars and prices

We built Findability because dealers asked to be findable in both classic search and the new AI answers, with copy that still sounds like the store.

## How it's delivered and how often
Site files and pages on the dealer website, then upkeep when facts, hours, or programs change. Not a one-time audit PDF.

## Outcome for the dealer
When someone asks a map, a search box, or an assistant about your brand in your city, there is accurate, current store content to cite — instead of a thin page or a competitor’s.

## FAQs
- **Is this just adding an llms.txt?** That’s one piece. The pages, the facts, and the files have to agree.
- **Will you list our cars and payments in the AI file?** No. We point to the live search and offer pages for current numbers.
- **Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?** No.
- **Can this stand alone?** Yes. It also sits in Director as the baseline for how the site and assistants describe the store.
