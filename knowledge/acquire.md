# Acquire

**Product name:** Acquire  
**One-line pitch:** A sales opportunity desk that turns today’s service drive and your customer relationship management (CRM) book into people to talk to — with a stock number that might fit.

## Who it's for
Owners, general managers, and salespeople at automotive rooftops who want a morning list, not another report. Built because dealers asked for a way to work the drive and the book in one place.

## What's included
- Same-day and near-term service appointments as the hero
- CRM warmth (who’s already in the funnel) joined to the person in the drive when the data allows
- New-car stock suggestions from the car in service
- A salesperson’s own book on the floor; managers see the full board
- Optional modules on the same desk (text wedge, internal brand-knowledge drawer) when the store uses them

## How it's delivered and how often
A gated web desk for that rooftop only. Refreshed on weekdays from the store’s service and CRM exports. Logins are per person; one store per URL. Not a public page.

## Outcome for the dealer
The floor gets a call sheet: who’s here or coming in, why they’re warm, who owns them, and which new units on the lot are a fit — so the drive and the book get worked the same morning.

## FAQs
- **Is this a CRM replacement?** No. It sits on top of the CRM and service schedule you already have.
- **Can shoppers or the website chat log into Acquire?** No. It’s an internal desk.
- **Will it text my customers from this call?** The phone assistant and website chat take messages for Lance. Acquire’s text tools, when a store uses them, are a separate, opted-in path — not this line.
- **Do you sell it only inside Director?** It can sit in the all-inclusive Director package or stand alone.
