# Products and services

The dealership offer is one system, handled by one team in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, for stores across the South.

- Search engine optimization (SEO), generative engine optimization (GEO) so AI assistants can read the site and recommend the store, and AI-written content checked by people who have worked dealership floors. Google Business Profile kept current.
- Luxury-lifestyle newsletters for each store, also published on the dealer website.
- Newsletter and special-offer emails (monthly specials, sales events, service and parts) that also live as pages on the dealer website, built to meet manufacturer brand rules.
- Custom photo, video and drone of the real lot, staff and inventory. No stock images. Reels, walkarounds, short-form video, and graphics that meet manufacturer brand rules.
- Social media on an in-house publishing system: posting rules by department (new cars, used cars, service, parts) for Instagram, Facebook and Google Business, timed to promotions, with a person reviewing every post before it publishes.
- Outsourced Chief Technology Officer (CTO) and Marketing Director. Vendor management as the single point of contact. Customer relationship management (CRM) and lead-flow fixes. A custom weekly report for the general manager (traffic, inventory, leads, response times and close rates) and a weekly sit-down on what to do next.
- Marketing spend review and manufacturer co-op claims, with creative built to qualify.
