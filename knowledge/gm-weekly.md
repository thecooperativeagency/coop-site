# GM Weekly

**Product name:** GM Weekly  
**One-line pitch:** A recurring pack for the general manager on whether the desk is taking care of the leads it already has.

## Who it's for
General managers and sales managers at a single automotive rooftop. One store, one URL — not a multi-store dashboard.

## What's included
- A store pack plus New and Used department views when the data supports it
- How leads arrived, how fast and how well they were worked, and who owns them
- Website traffic and lot health as context, not the headline
- Plain language for a dealer standup — no agency jargon

We built GM Weekly because managers asked for an honest read on lead handling, not another analytics tour.

## How it's delivered and how often
A private web pack for that store, typically at the start of the week and again mid-week. The primary window is the last thirty complete days compared with the prior thirty. Calendar dates are on the page. Lance forwards that store’s link — not a hub.

## Outcome for the dealer
The GM can see, in one place, whether internet leads are being contacted on time, where the desk is slow, and which people need attention — without mixing in other rooftops.

## FAQs
- **Is this the same as Acquire?** No. GM Weekly is a manager memo on lead handling. Acquire is a live sales desk for who to talk to today.
- **Will you email my whole group the same link?** No. Each store gets its own pack. We don’t send another rooftop’s page to your team.
- **Does it include customer names and phone numbers?** The GM pack is built for the desk, not for the public site. We don’t put customer identity on marketing pages.
- **Can you promise our contact rate will improve?** No. The pack shows what’s happening. What the desk does with it is the store’s.
