# Buyer's Guide

**Product name:** Buyer's Guide  
**One-line pitch:** Evergreen pages on your dealer site that answer lease, finance, cash, protection, warranty, tax, and certified pre-owned — in the shopper’s words.

## Who it's for
Automotive rooftops that want the website to explain the buying decision before the desk does, and to show up when people ask those questions in search or in an assistant.

## What's included
- A hub plus supporting pages (lease or finance, protection, lease-end, warranty and maintenance, certified pre-owned, tax and plates, trade-in or buyout, special programs)
- Copy that pairs city with state, spells things out, and stays evergreen — no rates, no expiration dates
- Questions and answers on the page that match the structured data
- Maintenance as the pages need it when the store’s facts change

We built these because dealers asked for a straight customer guide, not a specials page pretending to be education.

## How it's delivered and how often
Pages on the dealer website. Built once, then kept current. Not a monthly magazine and not a one-week offer.

## Outcome for the dealer
Shoppers can figure out lease vs finance vs cash, what protection is optional, and how tax works locally — then arrive with better questions. Search and AI tools have real pages to cite instead of a blank.

## FAQs
- **Does the guide quote monthly payments?** No. It explains how the choice works and points people to the store for numbers on their deal.
- **Is certified pre-owned instead of new?** No. It’s another way into the brand, not a vote against a new car or a lease.
- **Is protection required to get financed?** No. The guide treats those products as optional.
- **Can this live inside Director?** Yes, or à la carte.
