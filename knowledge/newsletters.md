# Letter

**Product name:** Letter  
**One-line pitch:** A monthly lifestyle magazine from your rooftop — local life, then the lot — not a specials blast.

## Who it's for
General managers and marketing managers at automotive dealerships (single rooftop or group) who want something customers actually read, in that store’s voice and city.

## What's included
- A full monthly issue in locked sections (open, brand, weekend, table, town, lot, service) written for that store only
- Photography from real people, real places, and your actual inventory — no stock
- A short email teaser that points to the live issue (some brands get the full letter in email instead of a site page)
- Proofing before anything is marked up for send

We built Letter because dealers asked for a customer piece that felt like the store, not another offer email.

## How it's delivered and how often
Monthly. The full issue lives on the dealer website when that brand uses a site page. Email is a teaser or the full letter, depending on the brand. Production is done before the month starts; send is the first of the month (or the following Monday if that falls on a weekend).

## Outcome for the dealer
Shoppers get a reason to open mail and visit the site that isn’t “this week’s rate.” The store shows up as a neighbor with a lot — one city, one mouth, no copy you could swap onto another rooftop.

## FAQs
- **Is this a sales email?** No. Specials never lead. The lot and service are in the issue; they don’t open it.
- **Can we use the same letter for every store in the group?** No. Each rooftop gets its own issue. If you can swap the store name, it isn’t done.
- **Do you write it from templates?** The bones are shared. The copy, photos, restaurant, and lot lead are that store’s.
- **Can the phone or chat quote what’s in this month’s issue?** No. Ask us to send a sample or talk through Letter with Lance.
